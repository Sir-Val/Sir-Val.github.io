Calculus

The mathematical language of change and accumulation — essential for every engineer and computer scientist. From limits to multivariable analysis.

Course Overview

Calculus is a foundational mathematics course required for all engineering and computer engineering students. This course develops analytical thinking and problem-solving skills by covering the core ideas of differential and integral calculus. Applications are drawn from physics, signal processing, optimization, and computational mathematics — directly relevant to engineering disciplines.

Topics Covered

  • Functions, Limits, and Continuity — epsilon-delta definition, one-sided limits
  • Derivatives — rules of differentiation, chain rule, implicit differentiation
  • Applications of Derivatives — related rates, optimization, curve sketching
  • Mean Value Theorem and Rolle's Theorem
  • Antiderivatives and Indefinite Integrals
  • The Definite Integral and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
  • Integration Techniques — substitution, integration by parts, partial fractions
  • Applications of Integration — area, volume, arc length, work
  • Sequences and Series — convergence tests, Taylor & Maclaurin series
  • Introduction to Multivariable Calculus — partial derivatives, gradient, divergence
  • Ordinary Differential Equations (Introduction)

Learning Outcomes

  • Evaluate limits and determine continuity of functions analytically
  • Compute derivatives and apply differentiation to optimization problems
  • Evaluate definite and indefinite integrals using various techniques
  • Apply calculus to engineering problems involving rates of change and area
  • Understand the convergence and use of power series expansions
