About Me

Engineering Expertise, Academic Heart

"I'm Val Patrick Fabregas — you can call me Sir Val." That's usually my opening greeting whenever I meet my students on the first day of class. Through decades of teaching in top Philippine universities, managing IT operations at enterprise scale, and now leading technology at an AI innovations firm — that title of "Sir" has become a mark of both mentorship and professional leadership.

A Computer Engineering graduate of the Technological Institute of the Philippines – Manila Campus (Batch 2000), I have built a career spanning IT service management, higher education, and technology consulting. I have completed the academic requirements for a Master of Engineering in Computer Engineering at Cavite State University – Main Campus, and hold dual Microsoft Technology Associate (MTA) certifications covering Windows Operating Systems and Computer Networks.

Currently serving as Director of Information Technology at Neuronet AI Innovations and as an IT Consultant at BCECS Advanced Computer Services, Inc., I bring over 25 years of progressive experience in IT service delivery, team leadership, quality assurance, academic instruction, and curriculum development across multiple prestigious institutions.
