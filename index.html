About Me

Engineering Expertise, Academic Heart

"I'm Val Patrick Fabregas — you can call me Sir Val." That's usually my opening greeting whenever I meet my students on the first day of class. Through years of teaching college, serving as a technical trainer in a BPO, and now leading IT operations as a Director — that title of "Sir" has become a mark of both mentorship and professional leadership.

I'm a Computer Engineering professional who thrives at the intersection of enterprise IT management, higher education, and emerging technologies like IoT and AI. Currently serving as Director of Information Technology at Neuronet AI Innovations in Bacoor, Cavite, I bring over two decades of progressive experience spanning IT service delivery, team leadership, technical training, and academic instruction.

My personal philosophy: interoperability — the ability to work with and adapt to different systems, teams, and challenges. Whether I'm managing an IT service desk, consulting on structural engineering software, or guiding students through algorithms and calculus, I engage with full commitment and adaptability.
